Stats on its own screen, and everything tappable

Stats leaves the Calendar / Stats switch: the Journal keeps only the calendar, and its month line ends with Stats ›, which opens Stats on that month (Back returns to the same day). In Stats every number, bar and row opens what it counts. Tap things in the middle phone: the purple line at the bottom says where each tap leads; the third phone shows one of them. Numbers are November's where I know them, the rest is sample.

1Journal: the way in

No more Calendar / Stats switch on top. The month line is a button: tap anywhere on it (or Stats ›) to open Stats for the month shown.

2Stats

Its own screen with ← back. Intervals / Gym / Both, then Week / Month / Year with ‹ › to go back in time. Tiles, bars, records and the most trained all tappable.

3Where a tap leads: "Gym days"

Tapping the Gym days tile lists those days, newest first, each with its strip and summary; a day opens the Journal on it. Other taps: a record opens the exercise's Records tab, an exercise its History, a bar the Journal on that day or week.
