Ideas from other apps

Every suggestion from the review of climbing, hangboard and gym apps, drawn on the app's real screens with your own data where it exists (Pinch, Squat variants, the “max hang 20mm” workout). Numbers on the cards match the list in the chat. Nothing here is built yet: pick the ones you want.

Best fits

1Suggest the next load

From Alpha Progression's double progression; “weight recommendations” is on the hangboard reviewers' wish list.

  • All reps done at the same load on the last two days → a teal hint under the tiles. Tap it to put 17 kg in the tile.
  • Intervals: the workout card and Finished say “try +11 kg” when every round was completed twice at +10.
  • Just a hint: no targets, no warnings, gone once you log a set.

2“Let go” during a hang

From Grips & Grades and the Hangboard app, which log success or failure for every hang.

  • During a work series, a Let go button under the disc. One tap marks that hang as failed; the timer keeps running.
  • Finished and the Journal then read “hangs 11/12” and say which one, instead of only “completed”.
  • Works with suggestion 1: a let-go hang means no “try more” next time.

3Progress for interval workouts

From Gneiss and Crimpd, whose charts are what reviewers like most. A Progress screen was in the first mockups and never built.

  • Opened from the workout card's ⋮ menu and from a session in the Journal.

4Gym rest clock on the lock screen

From Hevy's live notification (Android too). Intervals already does this for the interval player.

  • After a set, a notification shows the rest counting up, the target, the exercise and the next set.
  • It buzzes at the target and keeps counting, like the clock on the log screen. Tap = back to that exercise.
  • The rest lives in the notification's service, so it survives the app closing (fixes a known gap).

5Warm-up sets

From Hevy and FitNotes 2, where warm-up sets don't count in stats or records.

  • One more quick tag on a set: Warm-up (teal, first in the row).
  • Warm-up bars are drawn as dashed outlines and left out of records, set counts, volume and the “N sets” lines.
  • Optional per exercise: “first set of the day is a warm-up” — off by default.

8Finger load this week

From ClimbMax's overtraining flags and the finger-injury research (tracking load is the main prevention).

  • A Stats card from what you already log: hang time × load of interval workouts, plus sets of exercises tagged Fingers.
  • Shown as this week against your usual week (the average of the 4 before). Yellow above 130 %.
  • No new logging, no pain score, no advice text: just the number and where it came from.

10Climbing session log

From BetaFlow, SendLog and Boulder Log: grade plus flash / send / try, then best grade and flash rate.
