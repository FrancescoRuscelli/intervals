Move sets to another exercise

Today you logged four sets as Bulgarian Squat, but they were Squat. Three ways to start the move, then one picker for all three, then Undo. Merging a whole exercise (Finger Pinch into Pinch) stays where it is, in Edit exercise.

ASelected sets · Move
  • Hold a set, tap the others: the bottom actions get Move beside Note and Delete.
  • Moves exactly the sets you picked, also one set out of five.
BExercise menu · Move today's sets
  • ⋯ beside the name on the set screen: Move today's sets…
  • The common case, all of the day, in two taps; no selecting.
CGym day · hold the exercise
  • On the day page (also from the Journal), hold an exercise's block: Move to…
  • Fixes an old day too, without opening the exercise.
